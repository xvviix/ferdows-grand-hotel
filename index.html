The Residence

A house of slow mornings and long shadows

Since 1972, Ferdows Grand has been a refuge for travellers who measure a city by its light. Hand-cut travertine, walnut panelling and a courtyard garden of pomegranate and jasmine frame an experience of unhurried luxury in the capital.
