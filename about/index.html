Our Story

A House Built on Light

In 1972, the Ferdows family transformed a 19th-century Qajar-era garden house on the edge of old Tehran into a place of welcome. They kept the pomegranate trees, the tiled pools and the courtyard's quiet — and added a guesthouse of hand-cut travertine and walnut.

Five decades later, Ferdows Grand remains a family hotel in the truest sense. The original garden still anchors the property; the same standards of discreet, anticipatory hospitality guide a new generation of the family and a staff of two hundred.

What we hold dear

Craft

Materials chosen to age beautifully — stone, wood, silk and brass — maintained by artisans.

Discretion

Service that anticipates without interrupting. Our guests feel held, never watched.

Place

A deep respect for Persian gardens, ritual and hospitality — not as decoration, but as daily life.

Welcome

Two hundred staff, one intention: that every guest leaves lighter than they arrived.
