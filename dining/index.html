Tables of the Fertile Crescent

Four restaurants, a tea house and a candle-lit bar — a love letter to Persian cuisine and the wider region, crafted with modern discipline.

Fine Persian dining

Golestan

Our signature room. Kashan rosewater, saffron rice mountain, slow-braised lamb — presented in a glass hall overlooking the courtyard.

Signature: Rose-petal lamb shoulder

Regional & Caspian

Zayandeh

Caspian sturgeon, herb-laden kuku, and pomegranate-walnut stews served beside a tiled reflecting pool.

Signature: Charred sturgeon, barberry glaze

Grill & courtyard

Shiraz Garden

Open-air dining among jasmine and orange trees. Wood-fired koobideh and mountain lamb in the open air.

Signature: Saffron koobideh platter

International & pastry

Atelier

A bright, all-day brasserie and patisserie where French technique meets Persian citrus and pistachio.

Signature: Pistachio & rose mille-feuille

Daily

The Chaikhaneh

A quiet Persian tea house serving saffron tea, brewed in tall glasses over a samovar, with saffron-rose halva and dried figs.

Daily

The Cellar Bar

A candle-lit, wood-panelled room for long evenings — rare vintages, pomegranate aperitifs, and conversation.
